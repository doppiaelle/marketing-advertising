What it manages

From an Excel export to a closed finding.

Import

Bring in a scanner export or an Excel sheet of findings as-is — no manual re-typing of what an audit already produced.

Ownership

Every finding gets assigned to the team that has to fix it — nothing sits unowned in a shared inbox.

SLA

Deadlines follow severity automatically, and what's overdue is impossible to miss — no spreadsheet formula to maintain.

Audit

Every status change and reassignment is logged, so "who knew what, when" has an answer during a review.

Why a bank security team runs it this way

Self-hosted

Containerized
