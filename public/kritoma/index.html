How it plays

Simple rules. Difficult decisions.

place
place

Two sectors, six slots

The field is divided into two three-slot Sectors. Each can hold a Place that strengthens only the fighters inside it. Where you place a fighter matters as much as which one you play.

5Mana

Every card can be Mana

Once per turn, a card goes face down into the Mana Zone. No card is ever dead, but every card used as Mana is one you chose not to play.

20victory points
CombatObjectives

Twenty victory points

Half come from combat and half from Objectives: secret conditions that reward a specific way of playing. Winning through only one route is not enough.
