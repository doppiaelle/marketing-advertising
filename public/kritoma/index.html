Twelve disciplines, each with its own color, its own instincts and its own way of winning. No deck can use them all — a deck is built from exactly three, and what you don't pick shapes your identity as much as what you do.

Extra deck

Reserves & objectives

Extra combatants held outside the main 60, plus the Objective cards that win half the game without a single attack.

Three Mini Decks of different disciplines make a 60-card Main Deck. A shared Personality trait — Strategic, Methodical, Ambitious and seven more — can run across all three, turning three separate identities into one working synergy. Victory comes at 20 life, split evenly between combat and Objectives.

How you play it

Built for the table, playable on a screen.

On paper

Physical cards

Standard 63×88mm card stock, print-ready sheets, a real rulebook. Games happen across a table, the way a trading card game should.

On screen

Online matches

The same rules, the same 60-card decks, played on a board that highlights legal moves as you make them — no rulebook required mid-game.

Kritoma is a DoubleL creative venture, still taking shape.

Four decks are playable today; the other eight disciplines are on the way. Playtesters and feedback from the table are what move it forward fastest.
