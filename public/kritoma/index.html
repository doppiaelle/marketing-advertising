PLACE / LOCAL EFFECT

Same fighter.
Different neighbourhood.

Two Sectors. One Place slot and three fighter slots each. In this example, the Place gives your fighters +1 ATK in its own Sector. Placement is part of the plan.

Places add power, never extra slots.
INSTANT ACTION

One card.
Two new problems.

This Instant Action summons two 1/1 tokens. It resolves during Main Phase, then goes straight to the Graveyard. The three Action slots are for Continuous and Set Actions; this Instant Action never occupies one.

Newly summoned tokens wait until a later turn to attack.
EXTRA FIGHTER / SACRIFICE

Sometimes less
is a lot more.

In this example, one Creative fighter and two tokens pay the three-material requirement. The fighter goes to the Graveyard; the tokens disappear. The Extra arrives from the Extra Deck, summons two tokens and gives your fighters +1 ATK.

Sacrifices award no victory points. Tokens occupy fighter slots, but have no personality.

Illustrative card types and concept art. Names and artwork are examples for this showcase; the game is in development.

A FEW
OPEN CARDS.

What is Kritoma?

A trading card game built around twelve real-world disciplines. Combine Mini Decks, deploy fighters into two Sectors and pursue victory through combat, card effects and secret Objectives.

Are all twelve worlds ready to play?

Twelve worlds define the game’s identity. Mini Decks, artwork and balance are at different stages of development. The world selector above explores the concept; it is not the game’s deckbuilder or a promise that every combination is currently available.

Can I play on this page?

This is the Kritoma showcase. The opening sequence illustrates card types and rules; it is not a playable match. The public game platform will be linked here when it is ready. For now, follow development and playtests with the community.

Physical cards or online play?

The project includes both printable cards and an online two-player application. The rules and card catalogue are being developed together. Join the community to follow what comes next.
