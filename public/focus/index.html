What it keeps track of

Four routines, one conversation.

Notes

Say a thought once, mid-day, mid-thought. Focus files it where it belongs instead of leaving it in a chat you'll never scroll back to.

Shopping

Mention what you're out of, or what you need for the week. It lands on a running list, sorted, ready before you're at the store.

Finances

Log an expense the moment it happens, in a sentence. Focus tracks it against the shape of your month, no spreadsheet required.

Appointments

A date, a deadline, a plan mentioned in passing — it becomes an entry on your calendar, with nothing left to slip through.

How it works

You talk. Focus sorts it out.

01

Tell it, however you'd say it

No forms, no menus to hunt through. Write the way you'd text a friend — Focus reads intent, not syntax.

02

It's filed before you look away

Every message is parsed and routed to the right place — note, list, ledger or calendar — the moment it lands.

03

Find it again in seconds

Search across everything you've told Focus, or open the routine that matters right now — shopping, agenda, finances.
