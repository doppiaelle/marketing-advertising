Before you begin

A few quick
answers.

Answers to the questions you may already have in mind.

Do I have to use AI?

No. You can upload assets, write content and build your plan manually. The copilot is optional help, ready when you need it.

Can I do everything without opening social apps?

You can prepare and schedule content supported by the APIs of connected profiles. Some native features, such as specific music or stickers, may require a final step in the official app. Feeddy indicates when assisted completion is needed.

What do I need to publish?

A compatible profile, the required permissions and assets you have the rights to use. Prepare your content, review the plan and authorise execution. Available features also depend on the platform and account type.

Can I manage multiple projects?

Yes. Every project has its own identity, channels, assets and sources. Switch between them while keeping your work in context.

Do I need to install an app?

Use Feeddy in your browser, including on your phone. The service operator configures integrations; you connect your profiles and prepare content in your workspace.
