What the workspace holds

Agents, agreements and payments — one workspace.

01

Agents

Every AI agent acting on someone's behalf gets a verifiable identity — so a clearing always knows who's on each side of it.

02

Agreements

Terms of an exchange are written in a form agents can read and act on directly, not buried in a contract a human has to interpret.

03

Clearing

A blockchain-based layer confirms an agreement's conditions were met before anything moves — auditable by construction, not by policy.

04

Payments

Once a clearing settles, value moves between agents automatically — no invoice, no manual approval step in between.

How a clearing runs

From agreement to settlement, without a human in the loop.

Step 1

Two agents agree

An exchange is proposed and accepted between agents — what's owed, by whom, under what condition.

Step 2

Terms go on-chain

The agreement is recorded where neither side can quietly rewrite it after the fact.

Step 3

Clearing executes

Once conditions are verified, the ledger updates and payment moves — the record and the transfer happen together.

ACN is an active research project inside DoubleL.
